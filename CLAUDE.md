# Notes for Claude

## Kristi's design taste (for trend research)

- **Missoni is a favorite** for its color palettes and complex, layered colors. Always check Missoni when reporting on runway or color trends.
- **Chanel** often has major sea-change items. Check Chanel for pieces that signal a shift.
- **Calvin Klein's minimalism** is her preference. Say so plainly when maximalism is genuinely important in a season.
- **Fashion weeks to cover:** New York, Milan and Paris only.
- **Goal of trend research:** translate fast fashion trends into wall art, which is a bigger purchase with a slower trend cycle. Watch for shifts in shape language, for example when circular and oblong mid-century modern shapes give way to something else.
- **Only forward-looking trends.** Leave out anything that has already peaked, even if it is still selling. As of 2026 she considers bows and scallops dated (last year's look).
- **Holiday trends:** look at Neiman Marcus (including Horchow), Saks, Bergdorf, Pottery Barn, Crate & Barrel and Williams Sonoma, plus J.Crew and Ralph Lauren. Target and Walmart are too basic.
