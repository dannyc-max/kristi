Ridgeline bookmarks

YOU NEED

  • 5 fabrics, about 6½″ square each: 1 sky + 4 ridges (scraps are perfect)
  • Backing, 6½″ square: felt, canvas or old denim
  • Glue stick: Elmer's or a pattern/fabric glue stick

 

  • Thread to match, or one variegated spool
  • Machine with zigzag, or a hand needle
  • Paper & fabric scissors, ruler, pencil
  • Tip: dark ridges at the bottom, light at the top

HOW TO MAKE IT

  1. 1

    Cut the pattern, top down. Cut on ridge line 2; the lower piece is the template for fabric 2. Cut it on line 3 for fabric 3, and so on.

  2. 2

    Cut the fabric. Pin each template to its fabric and cut along the ridge edge. Cut the sky and backing 6½″ square.

  3. 3

    Glue the layers. Glue the sky to the backing, then ridges 2–5, back to front. Thin glue, kept ¼″ from each ridge edge.

  4. 4

    Let it dry completely before sewing, so the needle doesn't gum up.

  5. 5

    Stitch each ridge ⅛″ below its edge: straight or zigzag by machine, or running stitch, backstitch or whipstitch by hand.

  6. 6

    Trim, cut, zigzag. Trim to 6″ square, cut on the blue lines, then zigzag all edges of each bookmark so they won't fray.
