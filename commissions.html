Made to order · any custom work

Custom work, commissioned

If it’s made of cloth, ask. Art quilts, garments, pattern drafting, alterations, home linens and one-off sewing projects, made in the house.

  1. Consult

    We talk through the idea, sizes, fabric and timeline. Two consultations are included.

  2. Deposit

    The deposit is the cost of the fabric and materials, paid before anything is cut.

  3. Make

    Clothing is billed by the hour in the atelier. Other pieces get a quote. Either way, you get an estimate up front.

  4. Fit

    Up to two fittings are included for anything that’s worn.
